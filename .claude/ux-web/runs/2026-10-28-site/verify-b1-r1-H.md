### Regressions

**R1H-1 · Severity 2 · The listening circle looks like a button but does nothing**
- **Where:** `src/styles/voice.css:307-320` (`.voice-level`) and `src/components/VoiceDemo.tsx:965-973`, on `/`, `/es/` and `/ca/`, every viewport, light and dark. Renders `voice-recording.en.mobile.light.view.png` and `voice-recording-50s.en.desktop.dark.panel.png`.
- **The moment:** “I tapped the microphone to start, so I'll tap it again to stop.” Nothing happens.
- **What happens:** while listening, the circle is a filled green 80 px disc with a white microphone and a mint halo. That's the idle “Tap to talk” button, pixel for pixel, in the same place. Done sits about 90 px lower. Before this change, the microphone became Done in place, so a second tap on the same spot stopped the recording.
- **Why it fails:** the app draws its version deliberately as “a mic that isn't a button”: an outline `mic_none` icon in the primary colour inside a thin level ring (`../TaskFlow/App/lib/screens/voice_task_screen.dart:2745-2776`). guidelines.md signifiers: something that isn't a control shouldn't look like the control it replaced. Funnel continuity: the site should look like the app.
- **Fix (CSS only):** make the circle read as a picture. In `.voice-level`, `background: var(--mint); color: var(--green);`, keeping the halo, or a transparent fill with a 2 px `var(--green)` ring. Optionally swap the icon for an outline microphone if `Icon` has one. No new strings. It stays out of the focus order.
- **Verify:** re-render `voice-recording.*`; the circle no longer matches `voice-capture`'s record button. Its fill is not `var(--green)`. A first-time tester on a phone stops a recording with Done on the first try.
- **Evidence:** seen in current renders and verified in code. The tap behaviour is inferred.
