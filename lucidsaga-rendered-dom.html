01How it plays

You choose what happens. The world answers.

  1. 01

    Pick a world, or write one line

    Start from one of 23 worlds, or type a premise. The storyteller drafts the world and your character, and you change anything you like before you begin.

  2. 02

    Do anything

    Tap a suggested choice, type what you do, or say it out loud. Wait and watch, or act. There are no wrong moves, only consequences.

  3. 03

    Watch it play out

    The narration appears as it's written, step by step from where the last scene ended. Key moments get a picture, and any scene can be read aloud.

  4. 04

    The world remembers

    Every person, place, wound and promise is kept by the story engine. The dead stay dead, and every deed leaves a mark.

02What makes it different

A storyteller with a memory

Pictures at the moments that matter

A duel at dusk, a deal in a back booth: the storyteller draws the scenes that count, in one cinematic style. Each character keeps the same face from scene to scene.

Every scene, read aloud

Press play and a studio voice reads the scene in the story's own language and accent: Spanish from Spain, Catalan, or English in a native voice. Four voices to choose from, or let your phone read it for free.

Logic first, luck second

Outcomes follow cause and effect. A clever plan can beat a stronger foe, and dice decide only what the facts leave open.
